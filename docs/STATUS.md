**Infrastructure:** `docs/INFRASTRUCTURE.md` — services, how they connect, every environment variable, and how to deploy from zero.

**What AI does:** turns meeting notes or transcripts into tasks (a recording's transcript, made with Groq or another speech-to-text service, becomes notes only after an admin checks and saves it) (Claude, structured output, validated server-side). Every AI-created task is marked AI, shows the quote it came from and waits for the assignee to confirm, change or delete it (an admin can switch that waiting off on Admin → Settings, so the tasks count at once). Members can also ask a question about a meeting they were invited to (or a date): Claude answers only from that meeting's stored notes, in the language of the question, quotes the lines it used (checked against the notes) and says when the notes don't contain the answer. Optional: off without `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`.
