**Finished:** email/password sign-in (argon2id, database sessions, rate limiting), one-time setup/reset links, member management for admins (add, edit, deactivate/reactivate), `create-admin` bootstrap command, demo mode for the sandbox, route protection (`/admin/*` admin-only with a 403 page), shared layout with member and admin navigation. Members table and roster seed. Tasks: every member creates tasks and edits, hands over or finishes their own (admins all), full history; two-way Google Sheets sync (id column, three-way merge, app wins conflicts) running in the background with "Synced N s ago" and Sync now; task list with My tasks / All tasks and type/status filters, task detail with history, and admin task editing. Hours: Start/Stop timer (on Hours and the dashboard), manual entries and corrections, weekly progress against the tier commitment, recent weeks, admin "who is behind / working now" with per-member history and editing; every finished entry is mirrored to the "Ure dela" sheet tab (tab created if missing; failures shown with the exact error and a retry); hours.xlsx seeded as history. Meetings: pulled automatically from Google Calendar every 15 minutes via the secret iCal address (or imported from an .ics file; recurring events, time zones, cancellations; meetings keep their history when Google re-IDs them), invitees matched by email, member list of their meetings with check-in (present/remote) during the meeting, excuses with a reason, automatic "unexcused", history and attendance rate, check-in card on the dashboard; admin import report, per-meeting recording ("mark the others present"), member × series attendance table; email reminders (default 1 day and 10 minutes before, chosen per member) sent by a scheduled job; meetings.ics and attendance.xlsx seeded.
